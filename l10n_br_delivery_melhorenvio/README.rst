This module ships through `Melhor Envio <https://melhorenvio.com.br>`__,
the Brazilian shipping broker:

- the checkout offers one delivery option per Melhor Envio service
  (Correios PAC, Sedex, Jadlog, Loggi and so on), each with its real
  price and estimated delivery time, so the customer picks the best one;
- validating the delivery order buys the shipment (paid with the Melhor
  Envio wallet), generates the transport label and attaches its PDF to
  the picking, ready to print and hand to the drop-off point;
- the access key of the authorized NF-e is sent with the shipment; a
  delivery without NF-e goes out as non commercial, with the declaração
  de conteúdo;
- the tracking events feed the delivery states of the ``delivery_state``
  module, whose scheduled action keeps the pickings up to date and can
  notify the customer on delivery.

Configuration
=============

1. Create a delivery method in *Inventory > Configuration > Delivery
   Methods* with provider *Melhor Envio*, and fill the access token
   (Painel Melhor Envio > Integrações > Tokens de acesso) and the
   dimensions of the box the orders ship in. The *Environment* button
   switches between the sandbox and production.
2. Press *Synchronize Melhor Envio Services*: one delivery method is
   created per service.
3. Publish on the website the services the customer may choose.

The quote weighs the products of the order (minimum 50 g), so set the
weight on the products. Keep balance in the Melhor Envio wallet: the
shipment is bought when the delivery order is validated.

Usage
=====

At the checkout the customer sees one option per published Melhor Envio
service, with the price and the estimated delivery time for their ZIP
code.

Validating the delivery order buys the shipment and attaches the
transport label PDF to the picking: print it, stick it on the package
with the *DANFE Simplificado - Etiqueta* of the NF-e (see the
``l10n_br_nfe`` module) and hand the package at the drop-off point. The
tracking number fills in as soon as the carrier assigns it, and the
*Update delivery states* scheduled action follows the delivery.

Cancelling the shipment at the picking also cancels it at Melhor Envio.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- OAuth application flow (authorization code and refresh), besides the
  personal access token.
- Webhooks of Melhor Envio pushing the tracking in real time, instead of
  the polling of the scheduled action.
- Quote each product with its own dimensions (today the order ships in a
  single box configured on the delivery method).
